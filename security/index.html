Product security

Coordinated vulnerability disclosure policy

We welcome reports of potential security vulnerabilities in CortiPix software and device firmware.

Report a vulnerability

Email security@cortisci.com. Please include:

  • The affected product and software or firmware version, if known.
  • A description of the issue and its potential impact.
  • Steps to reproduce it, including relevant configuration and supporting evidence.
  • A way to contact you for follow-up, if you wish.

Use synthetic data in examples. Do not send patient information, passwords, or other sensitive personal data.

What to expect

We will acknowledge your report within five business days of receipt. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays, in Pacific Time.

We will assess the report and provide updates at least every 30 calendar days while it remains under investigation. These are communication commitments, not promises to resolve a vulnerability within those periods.

We coordinate public disclosure with the reporter case by case, considering severity and remediation progress. Please coordinate with us before publishing vulnerability details so we can investigate and address the issue.

With your permission, we may acknowledge your contribution in the relevant security advisory.

Testing boundaries

  • Test only equipment you own or have explicit permission to test.
  • Use a nonclinical environment, disconnected from patients and clinical networks.
  • Use synthetic data rather than patient information.
  • Stop if testing could affect patient safety or expose sensitive information.

We also accept reports of vulnerabilities accidentally discovered during normal clinical use. These testing boundaries do not prohibit reporting such discoveries.

Good-faith research

CortiSci will not pursue legal action against researchers for good-faith security research conducted in accordance with this policy. This commitment applies only to CortiSci and does not authorize testing of third-party systems.
